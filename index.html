Questions

Before you trust it with 7 a.m.

What is WakeBuddy?

WakeBuddy is a talking alarm clock app for iPhone. A character wakes you up with their voice instead of a ringtone, gets tougher every time you snooze, and stops only when you say your wake-up phrase out loud.

Does it ring in Silent mode and Focus?

Yes. WakeBuddy uses the iPhone alarm system (AlarmKit), so alarms ring in Silent mode and during Focus, like the Clock app. Open the app once after installing and allow alarms.

Is my voice recorded?

No. WakeBuddy listens only while an alarm is ringing, to check your phrase. Speech recognition runs on your iPhone, and audio is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

What if I can’t talk out loud?

Type the phrase instead. If you really need to, there’s also an emergency off.

What happens when I snooze?

You get five more minutes. Then the character comes back louder and less patient, and each round is tougher than the last.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Japanese, Spanish and German. Characters, phrases and jokes are written and voiced for each language.

How much does it cost?

WakeBuddy is free with one alarm and Sarge. Premium unlocks every character, unlimited alarms and your own voice: weekly, yearly with a 7-day free trial, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Which iPhones are supported?

Any iPhone that runs iOS 26 or later.

How is it different from other alarm apps for heavy sleepers?

Many hard-to-dismiss alarms make you solve math or scan a barcode. WakeBuddy makes you talk instead: a character you actually want to answer and a phrase you say out loud. It’s meant to be funny, not punishing.
