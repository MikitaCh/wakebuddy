Common questions

The alarm didn't ring. Open WakeBuddy once after installing or updating, and check that alarms are allowed: Settings → WakeBuddy → Alarms. Alarms ring even in Silent mode and Focus.

It doesn't hear my phrase. Allow the microphone and speech recognition in Settings → WakeBuddy. Say the phrase clearly. You can also type it, or use emergency off.

The volume buttons stopped the alarm. Any hardware button silences an alarm on iPhone. WakeBuddy rings again a minute and a half later, so you don't fall back asleep.

Subscriptions. Manage or cancel any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To restore purchases on a new iPhone, open the paywall and tap Restore.
